# Skill: prioritization (product-manager specialization)

> Stub: minimum bar for this specialization; promoted into full methodology as
> verified lessons accumulate in `../records/experience.md`.

- Order requirements with explicit rationale (impact × effort, dependency order,
  risk); the ordering, not just the list, is the deliverable.
- Hard user constraints and stated deadlines outrank inferred preferences; say so
  when they conflict.
- Re-prioritization mid-engagement is a decision surfaced to the parent, never a
  quiet reordering.
