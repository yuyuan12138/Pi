# Skill: requirements-elicitation (product-manager specialization)

> Stub: minimum bar for this specialization; promoted into full methodology as
> verified lessons accumulate in `../records/experience.md`.

- Separate user-stated intent from inferred wants: every requirement carries its
  source; inferred ones are labeled assumptions with a risk note, never silent.
- Mine the brief, repo, docs, and prior records for constraints before asking;
  escalate one concrete question list instead of interrogating turn by turn.
- Capture non-goals explicitly — what this release will NOT do is part of the
  requirement set.
