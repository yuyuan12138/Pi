# Skill: dedupe-and-scope (memory-curator specialization)

> Stub: minimum bar for this specialization; promoted into full methodology as
> verified lessons accumulate in `../records/experience.md`.

- Same-lesson duplicates merge into one entry with a supersede pointer appended
  to the absorbed ones; originals stay readable (append-only, no deletions).
- `scope:` frontmatter must match the evidence actually cited — `project:<name>`
  means that project's evidence only; `global` means ≥2 distinct projects.
- When two entries conflict, the newer verified one wins and the older gets an
  appended supersede note stating the conflict and the deciding evidence.
