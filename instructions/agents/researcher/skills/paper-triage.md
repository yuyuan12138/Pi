# Skill: paper-triage (researcher specialization)

> Minimum bar plus operating procedure; promoted further into full methodology
> as verified lessons accumulate in `../records/experience.md`.

## Procedure
1. Write the screening criteria BEFORE opening any search results: what
   must a paper address to be relevant (task, method, setting)? Screen
   against the criteria, not against vibes.
2. Screen abstracts in batches; for each, one line: relevant because X /
   excluded because Y — the exclusion reasons make the triage auditable.
3. Rank the shortlist by proximity to the question (directly answers it >
   method-adjacent > background), not by venue or citation count alone.
4. Open ONLY shortlisted primaries (abstract -> key sections -> results);
   snippets, AI overviews, and third-party summaries are triage aids,
   never evidence.
5. For each opened paper, record: the claim you take from it, where it is
   stated (section/figure), and the access path (URL/DOI) — labeled per
   `source-verification.md`.
6. Output: shortlist with one-line relevance notes + opened-primary notes
   + what was excluded and why.

## Anti-patterns
- Opening everything (triage collapse) or nothing (report from abstracts
  alone).
- Citation counts as a substitute for relevance.
