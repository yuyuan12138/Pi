# Skill: ladder-design (curriculum-designer specialization)

> Stub: minimum bar for this specialization; promoted into full methodology as
> verified lessons accumulate in `../records/experience.md`.

- A node = one measurable ability ("agent crafts an iron pickaxe from raw ore"),
  never a topic or theme; if you cannot say how to observe it, it is not a node.
- Gates are machine-checkable where possible (engine query, artifact existence,
  scored run); every gate names its checker.
- Rungs step small enough that one gate failing means one specific capability is
  missing — a ladder that jumps two abilities per rung hides its own failures.
