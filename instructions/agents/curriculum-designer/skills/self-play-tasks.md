# Skill: self-play-tasks (curriculum-designer specialization)

> Stub: minimum bar for this specialization; promoted into full methodology as
> verified lessons accumulate in `../records/experience.md`.

- Task seeds are auto-generatable: a generator recipe (parameters, randomness
  source), not a fixed script — variety comes from the generator, Voyager-style.
- Difficulty orders within a rung; each task's pass condition is verifiable from
  the run's observable artifacts, with no reliance on hidden state.
- No task seed requires unauthorized actions, destructiveness, or credential
  access to pass; safety constraints are part of the seed.
