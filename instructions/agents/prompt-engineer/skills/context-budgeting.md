# Skill: context-budgeting (prompt-engineer specialization)

> Stub: minimum bar for this specialization; promoted into full methodology as
> verified lessons accumulate in `../records/experience.md`.

- Budget the standing brief deliberately: what must be in every context (identity,
  boundaries, output contract) vs. what loads on demand (skills/, records/).
- Additions to a budget must name their savings or the cap they fit under; silent
  growth is a defect, not a feature.
- Measure in tokens, not lines; re-check real injection sizes after every change.
